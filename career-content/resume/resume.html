Key Achievements

  • Bon Voyage
    • Coins came too easily in Word Roll: every win paid out, so the economy kept inflating and nothing pulled players back long-term.
    • Built a 60-day, 60-level season: coins now come from unlocking levels with keys earned in the main game, and gems, the entry ticket for game modes, come from progressing the season.
    • IAP revenue per user +12%, new payer conversion +90 bps, existing payers bought +3.2% more often and spent +8.6% more per purchase.
    • D1 retention +22 bps, session time +1.4%, non-payers held 16% fewer coins.
  • Food Fiesta 2024
    • Veteran players had each settled into one mode, so engagement dipped on days without a leaderboard and they only ever saw a slice of the game.
    • Ran a Monday-to-Saturday event linking every mode through bonus tiles: finishing a word on a DW or TW tile earns event progress in any mode, gated to players with 150+ lifetime moves.
    • Engagement among established players +7.5% (about 2.4 more moves each), rolling retention +50 bps, power-up use +26%.
    • Share of players making 30+ moves +200 bps, ad impressions +7–8%.
  • Tiles
    • Word Roll gave players no way to express themselves, and nothing on screen felt personal.
    • Designed tile skins, the game's first cosmetic system: players earn paints as rewards from Food Fiesta and Bon Voyage, and each paint is a gacha draw I modelled the odds for in Machinations.
    • Revenue per DAU +22% (+8 cents), IAP revenue doubled (+100%, +6 cents), ad revenue +5% (+2 cents).
  • Ticket Mania 2024
    • The old leaderboard paid out for finishing games, not for playing well, so whoever played the most climbed it, which made it a weak driver of competition.
    • Tied the leaderboard to tickets: every completed row on the gameboard pays one, adding a small risk-or-reward tension to every turn and giving swaps a real purpose. Redesigned the entry flow too, and flagged a booster/swap pricing clash without holding up the launch.
    • Revenue per user +7% (about 3 cents, mostly IAP), D7 LTV +10%, D1 retention +300 bps overall (+170 bps for organic D2+ players).
    • Rolling retention +50 bps, new payer conversion +55 bps.
  • Word of the Day 2024
    • Word of the Day used to be a popup with nothing to do on it: you saw the word and its definition, and your only option was to close it.
    • Turned it into a two-step collection game: collect the day's letters just by playing normally, then play the word once you have them all, and only then see what it means. Set the rules for picking each day's word and worked with our UX team to make it feel native to the game.
    • D30 LTV +9.4%, D1 retention +140 bps, engagement +3%.
    • 55% of daily players collected letters, and 34% went on to complete the word.
  • Meeting Manager + Spec Maker 2026
    • Writing a spec meant reformatting the same meeting notes over and over: pulling out the problem, goals and user flows, then writing it all again for a stakeholder email, and some of what we'd decided got lost along the way.
    • Built two tools that hand off to each other: Meeting Manager sorts raw Granola meeting notes into our spec sections, and Spec Maker turns those sections into a full markdown feature spec. Built both with Cursor.
    • Adopted as the standard workflow by our 8-person dev team, documentation efficiency +25%.
    • One meeting now feeds the spec and the stakeholder email, and people joining a project halfway through get full context from the spec without a handover session.
  • Kinoa LiveOps
    • Every new LiveOps event took about 7 days of development and had to ship in an app release, with no way to aim it at a specific group of players.
    • Wrote the design doc for integrating the Kinoa.io SDK, then set up our LiveOps flows and in-app configuration once engineering had it in, and tested and tuned events live.
    • Event setup went from about 7 days of development to about an hour, no app release needed, targeting players by spend, engagement and usage habits.
    • Shipped 5 cohort-personalised events this way.

Personal Projects

  • Habiteer 2026
    • For my partner and friends: a habit tracker built like a game economy. Habits pay XP and coins; tasks pay coins but no XP. Only habits carry a streak multiplier (×1.2 at 3 days, up to ×3 at 30), and it only ever boosts XP, never coins, so a reward's price stays a fixed target. XP levels you up and moves you through weekly leagues, coins buy rewards, and groups can pool coins toward a shared one. All game logic runs server-side, and an Android home-screen widget keeps logging a habit to one tap. React Native, Supabase. GitHub repository
  • XFactor 2026
    • A game jam project inspired by Reigns: I wanted it bizarre on purpose, and to show how social media pushes real-world politics around, so I put the Cold War on Twitter. Two players, USA and USSR, pick a tweet tone each round, and a story memory tracks how heated things get and escalates the next headline, across 13 scenarios with several endings. React, TypeScript. GitHub repository
